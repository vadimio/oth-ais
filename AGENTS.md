## Documentation tense

- Use present tense for current-release features and work underway: "We are implementing A" or "The service does A"
- Reserve future tense for later-release, deferred or on-hold work: "In a later release, we will implement A"
- Record implementation, deployment and test status separately and accurately; present-tense design describes the release's behavior, while completed claims require evidence
- Preserve historical records, quotations and license wording
